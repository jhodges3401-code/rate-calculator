FAQ

Does Proposeroo Desk take payments?

No. It creates the invoice PDF; you get paid however you already do (bank transfer, PayPal, Stripe link). That keeps it simple and fee-free.

Can I send quotes?

Yes. Create a quote with line items and tax, download the PDF, and convert it to an invoice when the client accepts.

What does HoneyBook cost?

Its pricing page lists Starter at $29/month, Essentials at $49/month and Premium at $109/month, plus payment processing fees. Check HoneyBook's site for current details.
