FAQ

Does it work offline?

Your data is stored in the browser, so nothing is sent anywhere. You need a connection to load the page.

Can I pin it like an app?

Yes. In Edge or Chrome, use the menu to install the site or create a shortcut on your desktop or taskbar.

How do I back up?

Settings & data → Download backup (JSON). Restore it on any computer. Time entries and invoices also export as CSV.
