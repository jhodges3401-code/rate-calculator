FAQ

Can I import my Harvest history?

Yes. Export a time report as CSV from Harvest and choose it in Settings & data. You see a preview first. Rows Harvest marks as invoiced come in as non-billable. Import is a Pro feature; the preview works on the free plan.

Is Harvest free?

Harvest lists a free plan with 1 seat and 2 projects on its pricing page. Paid plans are billed per seat. Check Harvest's site for current details.

Do I need an internet connection?

Only to load the page. Your data is stored in your browser and nothing is sent to a server.
